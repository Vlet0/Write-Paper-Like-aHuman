# Notice & Acknowledgments

`Write-Paper-Like-a-Human` synthesizes top-tier computer science / AI best-paper writing architecture with deep linguistic humanization and empirical discourse-level de-AI findings.

We gratefully acknowledge the following upstream projects, empirical corpora, and research foundations:

---

### 1. Best-Paper-Writing-Skill
- **Repository**: [gulucaptain/Best-Paper-Writing-Skill](https://github.com/gulucaptain/Best-Paper-Writing-Skill)
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Original Authors**: gulucaptain, Master-cai ([Research-Paper-Writing-Skills](https://github.com/Master-cai/Research-Paper-Writing-Skills), MIT), with research insights originating from Peng Sida's study notes ([learning_research](https://github.com/pengsida/learning_research)).
- **Contributions Integrated**:
  - Corpus analysis of 60 award-winning papers from CVPR, ICCV, and NeurIPS (2021–2025).
  - 8-Type Paper Routing taxonomy (L1 Method improvement to L8 Re-examination).
  - Claim calibration methodology and evidence-to-claim mapping.
  - Figure 1 typology and publication-grade TikZ/matplotlib styling templates.
  - CVPR/NeurIPS author-kit LaTeX templates and exemplar bank (cards, moves, paragraphs, phrasebank).

### 2. Humanizer
- **Repository**: [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer)
- **License**: MIT License (Copyright (c) 2025 Siqi Chen)
- **Sponsor**: [cfo.ai](https://cfo.ai)
- **Contributions Integrated**:
  - The 26 core anti-AI tell patterns distilled from Wikipedia's WikiProject AI Cleanup ("Signs of AI writing").
  - The 4-step transformation pipeline (Mark tells -> Draft rewrite -> Check draft -> Final state).
  - Strict fact preservation invariant (retaining all numbers, names, claims, and causal relationships).
  - Sentence length burstiness and rhythm engineering.
  - Elimination of universal connectors, forced triads, puffed significance, and robotic residues.

---

### 3. StoryScope: Investigating Idiosyncrasies in AI Fiction (COLM 2026)
- **Authors**: Jenna Russell, Rishanth Rajendhran, Chau Minh Pham, Mohit Iyyer, John Wieting (University of Maryland, College Park & Google DeepMind).
- **Publication**: Conference on Language Modeling (COLM 2026), arXiv:2604.03136v6.
- **Contributions Integrated**:
  - Empirical proof that surface-level lexical/syntactic humanization is insufficient to escape AI detection or achieve authentic human evaluation, because detectors and human readers evaluate **discourse-level narrative architecture**.
  - Elimination of AI-elevated discourse patterns:
    * *Thematic Over-determination & Moralizing* (over-explaining significance and preaching paper importance).
    * *Structural Streamlining & Frictionless Progress* (glossing over real-world technical trade-offs and constraints).
    * *Lack of Authentic Operational Agency* (substituting vague passive system illusions for concrete algorithmic operators).
    * *Discourse Space Clustering* (falling into stereotypical rhetorical corridors across model families).
  - Integration of human-elevated discourse strengths:
    * *Intertextual Richness & Concrete Attribution* (naming exact prior mechanisms).
    * *Authentic Technical Friction* (demonstrating why obvious solutions fail with quantified cost).
    * *Non-Linear Insight & Delayed Recontextualization* (structuring the technical epiphany so it recontextualizes the bottleneck).
